Answers to the Most Common Questions

How long does a detail take?
A Basic Exterior takes about 1.5 to 2 hours. Premium and Elite packages run 2.5 to 3.5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. Elite Interior on a heavily soiled vehicle can run up to 4 hours. You'll get a realistic time estimate at booking.
Do you come to me, or do I bring the car somewhere?
We come to you. Give us your address at booking and we show up at your home, driveway, or workplace. No drop-off, no waiting around at a shop.
What's the real difference between Premium and Elite?
On exteriors, Elite adds Koch Chemie ProtectorWax and a Suds.lab CP Ceramic Wash rinse that gives your paint actual hydrophobic protection. On interiors, Elite adds full Bissell extraction on every carpet and upholstery surface, Koch Chemie Leather Star conditioning on all leather, and Koch Chemie Top Star dressing on every plastic and rubber surface. Premium hits the stains. Elite hits everything. If your car hasn't been detailed in a while, Elite is the right call.
What happens if I'm not happy with the result?
Send a photo showing what was missed. We'll schedule a return visit at no charge. No pushback about whether it qualifies. If we missed something, we come back and fix it. That's the guarantee on every service.
Do you offer anything for regular customers?
We're working on recurring packages for clients who want to keep their car on a regular schedule. Reach out directly after your first booking and we'll put something together that works for you.
Are you insured?
Yes, we're licensed and insured. If you want specifics before booking, call us at (248) 877-9060 and we'll answer any questions directly.
