Interior
Maintenance Interior
About 1 hour

Maintenance picks up where a full detail leaves off. Once we have brought your interior back with a Premium or Elite, this is how you keep it that way.

Built for cars that are already in good shape. If we have detailed yours before, you are set. If not, send a few photos of the inside and we will tell you whether it is ready for Maintenance or wants a full detail first.
Air Blowout, Vents and Crevices
Compressed air through every vent, seam, button and seat rail first, before anything else. Loose grit gets driven out rather than pushed deeper, which is why this is step one and not step five.
Full Vacuum
Seats, carpets, mats and trunk, worked top to bottom while the debris is still loose from the blowout. Crevice tool down the seat rails and between the cushions.
Surface Wipe Down
Koch Chemie Pol Star on the softer surfaces and door panels, Top Star on the heavy plastic. Sprayed onto the cloth rather than onto your screens.
Interior Glass and Mirrors
Sprayway on every interior window and mirror, two cloths, one to clean and one to buff. Ammonia free, so it is safe on tinted glass.
Final Walkthrough
A last pass over the whole interior before we hand it back. If something is not right, it gets done again before we leave.
What this is not. Maintenance is a one hour visit to keep a clean car clean. No stain work, no carpet extraction, no leather treatment. If the car needs more than an hour, it is a Premium Interior.
Subscription only, billed monthly. Prices vary with vehicle size and condition. Send photos of the inside and we will give you an accurate quote.

Interior
Premium Interior
2 to 3 hours

The full interior clean. Blown out, vacuumed, mats out and scrubbed, every surface done by hand, and spot treatment on the marks that bother you.

Air Blowout, Vents and Crevices
Compressed air through every vent, seam, button, cup holder, door pocket and seat rail before anything else. Everything loose comes out now, so the vacuum picks it up instead of burying it.
Full Vacuum
Seats, carpets, trunk and mats, top to bottom, straight after the blowout while it is all still loose. Crevice tool along the rails and down between the cushions.
Floor Mats, Out and Scrubbed
Mats come out of the car, get scrubbed with Koch Chemie Top Star, and go back only once they are dry. Mats left in the car never get properly cleaned.
Detail Brush Work
Brushes into the vents, buttons, knobs and seams that air alone will not clear, with Pol Star as the agitation spray. This is the part that makes an interior look cared for rather than just vacuumed.
Deep Surface Clean
Koch Chemie Pol Star across the softer surfaces and door panels, and Top Star on the heavy plastic where it brings the finish back. Dashboard, console, door pockets, cup holders, center stack, seat backs. Top to bottom, sprayed onto the cloth rather than onto your screens.
Leather Conditioned
Koch Chemie Leather Star worked into the leather seats, steering wheel and armrests with an applicator, then buffed off with a clean towel. It keeps the leather supple and helps stop it drying out and cracking.
Spot Treatment on Carpet and Upholstery
Koch Chemie Pol Star worked in with the drill brush on visible marks, then the extractor over the same area while it is still wet. Pol Star is pH neutral, so it lifts the dirt without stripping the fabric. Targeted at the spots that bother you, not the whole floor.
Steam on Stubborn Marks
Steam into the spots and seams a brush cannot reach. Heat breaks down what agitation alone leaves behind.
Door Jambs Cleaned
The channels your doors close into, which almost nobody cleans. Road grime, salt and black streaks come off, and it is the first thing you notice every time you open the door.
Seat Belts Wiped Down
Pulled all the way out, wiped, and let retract dry. Nobody expects this one.
Interior Glass and Mirrors
Sprayway on all interior glass, two cloth method, streak free and safe on tint.
Odor Treatment
OdoBan misted lightly through the cabin. A light mist, not a soaking.
Final Quality Inspection
We go back over every area before we hand the car back. Nothing leaves until it meets the standard, and if you spot something after, tell us and we come back and fix it at no charge.
What this is not. Spot treatment handles marks, it is not a full carpet extraction.
If the carpets are stained, or the interior has not been properly cleaned in a couple of years, Elite Interior is the one you want.

Interior
Elite Interior
2.5 to 4 hours

Everything in Premium, then further. Seats moved for the rails underneath, and every carpeted section worked rather than just the marks.

Everything in Premium Interior
Every step above, then the work below on top of it.
Seats Moved, Rails and Underneath
Seats slide all the way forward, then all the way back. Compressed air and vacuum under and behind both rows, and brushes down into the seat rail tracks. The places that never get touched on a normal clean.
Dry Agitation Before Any Water
The carpet gets brushed and vacuumed dry, twice, before anything wet goes near it. Most of what is in a carpet comes out dry, and pulling it out first means less water going in and a car that comes back drier.
Full Carpet and Upholstery Work
Every carpeted section gets Pol Star and drill brush agitation, then the extractor worked across it panel by panel with overlapping passes, rather than only on the marks.
Headliner Spot Cleaning
A barely damp cloth, very light pressure, on marks only. Headliners are glued fabric and they are treated with respect.
Interior Plastic and Rubber Dressed
Koch Chemie Top Star across the interior plastics and rubber, wiped to an even finish. Matte, not shiny.
Full Odor Treatment
OdoBan through the cabin, steam where it is needed, and the windows left cracked to air out before you get in.
Final Quality Inspection
The same last pass over everything, and the same promise. Not right, tell us, we come back.
What this is not. The extractor is a spot machine worked section by section, so this is a deep clean rather than a truck-mount extraction. Set-in stains improve, some do not come out completely.
Prices vary with vehicle size and condition. Send photos of the inside and we will give you an accurate quote.

Exterior
Premium Wash
1.5 to 2 hours

A proper hand wash. Foam pre-soak, two buckets, bug and tar off the front end, door jambs, and a protection layer over the top.

Pressure Pre-Rinse
The whole vehicle gets rinsed top to bottom first, so the heavy grit is off the paint before a mitt ever touches it.
Foam Pre-Soak
Koch Chemie Green Star through the foam cannon, worked in sections rather than the whole car at once, and given time to dwell. Foam lifts dirt so the wash is not dragging it across your paint.
Bug and Tar Treatment
Turtle Wax Bug and Tar Remover on the front bumper and lower panels while the foam dwells, then agitated and rinsed. It gets real dwell time and a second application if it needs it.
Wheels, Tires and Wheel Wells
Green Star on all four wheels, agitated with a woolie brush, then the wells done separately. Wheels always before the body, because wheel work splashes.
Two Bucket Hand Wash
Meguiar's Gold Class, one panel at a time, top to bottom, with the mitt rinsed in clean water between panels. Two buckets means grit goes in the rinse bucket instead of back onto your paint.
Full Rinse
Pressure rinsed, and the car stays wet for the next step.
Protection Layer
Koch Chemie ProtectorWax applied section by section over the wet car and rinsed as we go. It is a thin rinse-off sealant, so it goes on in sections rather than all at once.
Door Jambs Cleaned
Green Star, diluted down, wiped through the channels your doors close into. The bit that is always filthy and never cleaned.
Hand Dry
Dried by hand panel by panel, top to bottom. No blowers, no letting it air dry into water spots.
Exterior Glass and Mirrors
Sprayway, two cloths, one to clean and one to buff.
Trim Wiped
Trim wiped down as part of the finish.
Tire Dressing
Meguiar's Hot Shine Foam, last thing, kept off the paint. A clean satin finish rather than a greasy shine.
Final Quality Inspection
Checked from several angles before we finish, because marks hide in flat light. Anything wrong, tell us and we come back.
What this is not. This is a wash and a protection layer. It is not paint correction, and it will not remove existing scratches or swirls.

Elite Detail

Everything in Premium Wash, with a higher grade ceramic wash soap and the wheels done properly, barrels included.

Everything in Premium Wash
Every step above, with the two upgrades below.
Ceramic Wash Instead of Standard Soap
Suds.lab CP Ceramic Protective Wash replaces the Gold Class for this tier, through the foam cannon and the bucket. A higher grade wash soap with more lubricity, so the mitt glides instead of dragging.
Full Wheel Detail
Faces, barrels and wells, not just the faces. The woolie goes deep into the barrel where the brake dust actually lives. This is the part most washes skip entirely and it is the difference you see standing next to the car.
Final Quality Inspection
Same last pass, same promise.
What this is not. Still a wash, not paint correction. The ceramic wash is a soap, so it cleans and adds slickness, it is not a coating.

Answers to the Most Common Questions

How long does a detail take?
Maintenance Interior is about an hour. Premium Interior runs 2 to 3 hours and Elite Interior 2.5 to 4, depending on size and how bad it is. On the outside, Premium Wash is 1.5 to 2 hours and Elite Detail 1.75 to 2.5. Condition matters more than size, so a neglected sedan can take longer than a clean SUV. You'll get a realistic time estimate at booking.
Do you come to me, or do I bring the car somewhere?
We come to you. Give us your address at booking and we show up at your home, driveway, or workplace. No drop-off, no waiting around at a shop.
What's the real difference between Premium and Elite?
On interiors, Premium covers the full clean: blowout, vacuum, mats out and scrubbed, every hard surface, leather cleaned, spot treatment on marks, door jambs and seat belts. Elite adds the seats being moved for the rails and underneath, dry agitation before any water, the extractor worked across every carpeted section rather than only the marks, headliner spots, and the plastics dressed. On the outside, Elite swaps the soap for a ceramic wash and adds a full wheel detail with the barrels done by hand, which is the part most washes skip. Both get the same higher-grade soap. It is mostly extra hand time. On interiors, Elite is the bigger jump: the Bissell SpotClean gets worked across every carpet and upholstery panel instead of just the stains, plus deep seat rail cleaning, headliner spots, Koch Chemie Leather Star on all leather, and Top Star on every plastic and rubber surface. Premium hits the stains. Elite hits everything. If your car hasn't been detailed in a while, Elite is the right call.
What happens if I'm not happy with the result?
Tell us. Send a photo showing what was missed and we'll schedule a return visit at no charge. No pushback about whether it qualifies. If we missed something, we come back and fix it. That stands on every service.
Do you offer anything for regular customers?
Yes. Maintenance Interior is a monthly visit that keeps a clean car clean, about an hour each time. It is built for interiors that are already in good shape, so it picks up after a Premium or Elite rather than replacing one. If we have detailed your car before you are set, and if not, send a few photos and we will tell you whether it is ready for it.
Do I need to clean out my car before you get here?
Yes, and it matters more than most people expect. Empty the glove box, center console, cup holders, door pockets, seat backs, and trunk before your appointment. We clean around personal belongings rather than moving them, so anything left behind does not get cleaned under. Loose bottles and receipts are no problem. If there is more than a grocery bag's worth of trash, or any food waste or liquids, a $25 removal fee applies and we photograph it before anything is moved. Emptying the car means that area does not get cleaned. An empty car is the difference between a good detail and a great one. It also keeps your belongings your own, since we are not responsible for items left in the vehicle.
